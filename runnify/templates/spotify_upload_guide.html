Spotify Upload Guide

Set up your Spotify data with Runnify to unlock full music-performance insights.

1. Request your Spotify data

To get started, open the Spotify Privacy page and scroll down to “Download your data.”

  • Untick “Account data”
  • Tick “Extended streaming history”

Then press Request data. You’ll get a confirmation email shortly.

2. Confirm your request

Spotify will send an email asking you to confirm. Open it and click the confirmation link — this starts preparing your data for download.

(You’ll receive another email when your data is ready to download.)

3. Enable stream sync

Spotify’s exported files don’t include your most recent few days of listening. To avoid missing data, enable Sync recent streams in your Runnify Settings after making the request.

This ensures all new plays are saved while you wait.

4. Wait for your data

Spotify will gather your data — this can take up to 30 days.

“We're currently gathering a copy of your personal data. This shouldn't take longer than 30 days.”

5. Download and extract

When your files are ready, download the .ZIP file from Spotify and extract it. You’ll find multiple .json files containing your listening history.

⚠ Keep these files private — they contain sensitive account data.

6. Upload your files to Runnify

  1. Go to the Import page (/import).
  2. Log in to your account.
  3. Drag and drop your .json files, or click to select them.
  4. Press Upload to begin processing.

Runnify will process your files automatically in the background — you can safely close the tab while it works.

7. All set!

Once complete, you’ll unlock full song–performance insights:

  • Every song played during your runs
  • Performance scores per track
  • Personalised playlists for training and race day

Your future streams and runs will now sync automatically.
