How these are worked out {{ icon("chevron-down", class="insights__method-icon") }}

Each time a song plays during a run, Runnify compares your pace while it played with your pace in the five minutes before and after it, in the same run. That cancels out most of the warm-up, hills and tiredness, so what's left is mostly the song.

A song only ranks once it has played on two or more runs, and its lift is held back towards zero in proportion to how little evidence there is. A song with one great run can't top a song that has helped you on ten.

Moments when you stopped are ignored, and so is a song that played for less than about 20 seconds of running.
