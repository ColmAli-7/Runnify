Import your Spotify history

Spotify keeps a record of everything you've played. Runnify uses it to find the songs that played during your runs.

Get the file from Spotify

Spotify can take up to 30 days to send it

  1. Open Spotify's privacy settings

    Sign in at spotify.com/account/privacy (opens in a new tab) and find Download your data.

  2. Ask for your extended streaming history

    Tick Extended streaming history, not just account data, then request it and confirm from the email Spotify sends.

  3. Wait for Spotify's email

    It usually arrives within a few days and can take up to 30. Keep running with music in the meantime: your new runs are matched later.

  4. Download the zip and upload it here

    Upload the file exactly as Spotify sends it. There's no need to unzip it.
