    How a song gets its score

    Every song is measured against you, in the same run. Not against other runners, and not against a tempo chart.

    1. 1

      Your pace, second by second

      Runnify reads the pace your watch recorded every second. Moments when you stopped, at lights or for a breather, are left out.

    2. 2

      Your music, play by play

      Your Spotify history says when every song started and stopped, so each one lands on the exact seconds of the run it played in.

    3. 3

      Each song against the minutes around it

      A song is compared with how you ran in the five minutes before and after it, on the same kind of running, so a warm-up, a hill, tired legs or the reps of an interval session mostly cancel out.

    4. 4

      Ranked, with the evidence

      A song only ranks once it has played on two or more runs, and the less evidence behind it, the more its lift is held back. One lucky play can't top the chart.

    Set up once

    You'll need a Garmin watch and a Spotify account. The only slow part is waiting for Spotify to send your history.

    1. {{ icon("watch") }}

      Connect Garmin

      Sign in to Garmin Connect once. Runnify keeps an encrypted session token, never your password, and brings in each run with its second-by-second pace and heart rate.

    2. {{ icon("disc") }}

      Request your Spotify history

      Ask for your extended streaming history in Spotify's privacy settings. Spotify emails it to you, which can take up to 30 days.

      {{ icon("clock") }}Up to 30 days

    3. {{ icon("upload") }}

      Upload the zip

      Only the songs that played during your runs are kept, and the file is deleted as soon as it has been read. Your results appear when the matching finishes.

    {# ---- questions ---- #}

    Questions

    What do I need?{{ icon("plus", class="faq__icon") }}

    A Garmin watch that records your runs with GPS, the Garmin Connect account it syncs to, and a Spotify account you listen on while you run.

    Why does the Spotify history take so long?{{ icon("plus", class="faq__icon") }}

    Spotify only provides your extended streaming history on request, by email, and says it can take up to 30 days. Ask for it today and connect Garmin while you wait: runs you do in the meantime are matched when the file arrives.

    How is a song's lift worked out?{{ icon("plus", class="faq__icon") }}

    Your pace while the song played is compared with your pace in the five minutes before and after it, on the same kind of running in the same run: reps against reps, jogs against jogs. Any heart rate beyond what that extra speed normally costs comes off the lift. A song ranks once it has played on two or more runs, and its lift is held back towards zero in proportion to how little evidence there is.

    Does it work with Apple Music or other watches?{{ icon("plus", class="faq__icon") }}

    Not yet. Runnify reads runs from Garmin Connect and listening history from Spotify only.

    What does it cost?{{ icon("plus", class="faq__icon") }}

    Nothing. Creating an account and using Runnify is free.
