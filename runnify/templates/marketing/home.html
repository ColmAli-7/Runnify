Measured against your own running

A song isn't judged against other runners or a tempo chart. It's judged against you, a few minutes earlier and later in the same run.

  1. 1

    Your pace, second by second

    Runnify reads the pace your watch recorded every second. Moments when you stopped, at lights or for a breather, are left out.

    {{ run_chart(sample.stage_charts["pace"], "Pace through the sample run, with gaps where the runner stopped.", class="chart--compact") }}

  2. 2

    The song, and the minutes around it

    Each song is compared with how you ran in the five minutes before and after it, so a warm-up, a hill or tired legs mostly cancel out.

    {{ run_chart(sample.stage_charts["context"], best.song.name ~ " highlighted, with the five minutes either side of it shaded.", class="chart--compact") }}

  3. 3

    A result you can trust

    A song only ranks once it has played on at least two runs, and the fewer runs behind it, the more its lift is held back. One lucky play can't top the chart.

Read the privacy policy

  • {{ icon("lock") }}

    Your Garmin password is never stored. Runnify signs in once and keeps an encrypted session token instead.

  • {{ icon("music") }}

    Only songs from your runs are kept. The rest of your listening history is ignored, and the upload is deleted once it's read.

  • {{ icon("shield") }}

    No ads, trackers or third-party scripts. Every page, font and script is served by Runnify itself.

  • {{ icon("download") }}

    Your data stays yours. Download all of it, or delete your account, from Settings at any time.
