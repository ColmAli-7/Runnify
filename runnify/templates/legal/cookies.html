The short version

Runnify only uses the cookies it needs to keep you signed in and to protect your account. There are no analytics, advertising or third-party cookies.

Cookies Runnify sets

Every cookie Runnify uses
NameWhat it doesHow longType
runnify_session Keeps you signed in while you use the site, and protects forms against cross-site request forgery. Until you close your browser Essential
runnify_remember Keeps you signed in on this device. It is only set if you tick "Keep me signed in". 30 days, or until you sign out Essential, set at your request

Runnify doesn't keep anything else in your browser.

Why there is no cookie banner

Consent is only needed for cookies that aren't strictly necessary for a service you asked for. Both of Runnify's cookies are strictly necessary, so there is nothing to ask. If that ever changes, Runnify will ask you first.

Managing cookies

You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings. If you block runnify_session, you won't be able to sign in.
