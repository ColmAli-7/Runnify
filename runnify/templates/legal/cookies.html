The short version

Runnify only uses the cookies it needs to keep you signed in and to protect your account, and remembers the appearance you choose if you pick one. There are no analytics, advertising or third-party cookies.

Cookies Runnify sets

Every cookie Runnify uses
NameWhat it doesHow longType
runnify_session Keeps you signed in while you use the site, and protects forms against cross-site request forgery. Until you close your browser Essential
runnify_remember Keeps you signed in on this device. It is only set if you tick "Keep me signed in". 30 days, or until you sign out Essential, set at your request
runnify-theme Remembers whether you chose the light or dark appearance. It is kept in your browser's local storage, only when you pick Light or Dark, and is never sent to Runnify. Until you choose Auto or clear this site's data Set at your request

Runnify doesn't keep anything else in your browser.

Why there is no cookie banner

Consent is only needed for storage that isn't strictly necessary for a service you asked for. Runnify's two cookies are strictly necessary, and the appearance setting is only stored when you choose it yourself, so there is nothing to ask. If that ever changes, Runnify will ask you first.

Managing cookies

You can delete or block cookies and site data in your browser settings. If you block runnify_session, you won't be able to sign in. Choosing Auto under Appearance removes runnify-theme.
