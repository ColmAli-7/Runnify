What Runnify collects, and why

The data Runnify stores, what it is used for, and the legal basis
DataWhyLegal basis
Your name, email address and password To create your account and sign you in. Your password is stored only as an Argon2id hash, never as text. Contract
Runs from Garmin: start time, distance, duration, average heart rate and pace, and second-by-second pace and heart rate To measure how each song affected your running. Heart rate can count as health data, so Runnify asks for your explicit consent. Explicit consent
Your Garmin account email and Garmin session tokens To fetch your new runs. Runnify never stores your Garmin password. The tokens are encrypted. Consent
Songs from your Spotify history that played during a run: track, artist, start time, seconds played, and whether you skipped it To link songs to runs. Plays outside your runs are never stored, and the file you upload is deleted as soon as it has been processed. Explicit consent
Spotify connection tokens, if you connect Spotify To save playlists you build to your Spotify account. The tokens are encrypted. Consent
What Runnify works out: song effects, insights and playlists These are the service itself. Explicit consent
Friends and friend requests To show friends on the distance leaderboard. Friends see your name and total distance. Anyone signed in can find your name in search. Contract
Security log: sign-ins and account changes, with the network part of your IP address and a shortened description of your browser To protect your account and show you its recent activity. Entries are deleted after 90 days. Legitimate interests (keeping accounts secure)

What Runnify does not do

  • No advertising, and no selling or sharing of your data for marketing.
  • No analytics, tracking pixels or third-party scripts. Every page, font and script comes from Runnify itself.
  • No cookies beyond the essential ones in the cookie policy.

Who else handles your data

  • The hosting provider that runs Runnify and stores its database, on Runnify's behalf.
  • The email provider that delivers account emails, such as password resets.
  • Garmin and Spotify, but only when you connect them. Their own privacy policies cover the data they hold. Runnify is not affiliated with either.

If any provider handles data outside the European Economic Area, Runnify only uses providers that offer appropriate safeguards, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long data is kept

  • Your account, runs, songs, insights and playlists: until you delete your account.
  • The security log: 90 days.
  • Uploaded Spotify files: deleted as soon as they have been processed.
  • Password-reset links: they work once, for 30 minutes.

Deleting your account removes all of the above straight away. Copies in routine backups are overwritten on the hosting provider's normal schedule.

Your rights

  • Get a copy of your data: Settings, then Download my data. You get a JSON file you can keep or take elsewhere.
  • Correct it: change your name in Settings, or email us about anything else.
  • Delete it: Settings, then Delete my account. Everything is removed.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, by deleting your account or emailing us. This doesn't affect processing that happened before.
  • Object to or restrict processing by emailing {{ site.contact_email }}.
  • Complain to the Data Protection Commission (dataprotection.ie) or the data protection authority where you live.

How your data is protected

Passwords are hashed with Argon2id. You can turn on two-step verification. Garmin and Spotify tokens are encrypted. Connections use HTTPS. Sign-in attempts are rate limited, and accounts are locked after repeated failures. Pages only load code from Runnify itself.

Children

Runnify is not for anyone under 16.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that matters, Runnify will ask you to confirm the new version the next time you sign in.
